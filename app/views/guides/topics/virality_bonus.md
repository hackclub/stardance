# Virality Bonus

A critical part of the hacker spirit is actually shipping your project.
Now there's a bonus for how well you ship it to the wider world!

Get up to 5,000 bonus stardust :stardust: per viral project.

Ship your project, share it with the world, and if it hits a threshold on any platform, submit a claim at [hack.af/stardance-project-mention](https://hack.af/stardance-project-mention).
A reviewer verifies it, and you earn your bonus.

## ⭐ Good

Your project got noticed. **+50 stardust :stardust:** flat bonus, plus the Good Viral Project achievement and exclusive shop items.

## 🌟 Great

Your project blew up.
Payout ceiling goes from **20 to 30 stardust :stardust: per hour**.
Plus the Great Viral Project achievement and everything from Good.

## Thresholds

Hit one on one platform to qualify.

| Platform | ⭐ Good | 🌟 Great |
|---|---|---|
| Reddit | 25 upvotes | 500 upvotes |
| Hacker News | 10 points | 100 points (front page) |
| GitHub | 25 stars | 100 stars |
| YouTube | 250 views | 50,000 views |
| TikTok / Reels / Shorts | 5,000 views | 100,000 views |
| Twitter / X | 50 likes | 1,500 likes |
| Bluesky / Threads | 50 likes | 1,500 likes |
| Instagram | 200 likes | 100,000 views OR 10,000 likes |
| App store (Google Play/Apple) | 100 downloads | 10,000 downloads |
| Package manager (npm, PyPI, etc.) | 1,000 downloads | 50,000 downloads |
| Modrinth / CurseForge | 2,500 downloads | 100,000 downloads |
| Blog / news article | Any third-party feature | At our discretion |

Platform not listed? Post in [#stardance-help](https://hackclub.slack.com/archives/C0AP0NMSP3P) and we'll figure it out.
